# Discovery

## Target user
A student or young traveler planning a trip on their phone, comparing destinations and trying to decide where to go.

## How Might We
How might we help travelers quickly browse, compare, and shortlist destinations on any device without feeling overwhelmed?

## Empathy Map
| | |
|---|---|
| Says | "I don't know where to go." "Is this place worth the price?" "I'll check this on my phone later." |
| Thinks | "Too many tabs open." "Will I regret picking this place?" "Is this site even readable on my phone?" |
| Does | Scrolls on mobile, opens many tabs, screenshots places she likes, compares budgets and weather. |
| Feels | Overwhelmed by choices, excited about the trip, frustrated by cluttered or slow sites. |

## Pain points to solve
- Too many options: category filters and search
- Hard to decide: clear destination cards with key info up front
- Can't remember favorites: a save button
- Bad mobile experience: mobile-first layout and large tap targets
